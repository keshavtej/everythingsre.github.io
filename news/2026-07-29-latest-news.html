Observability · 28 Jul

Official project

OpenTelemetry Demo 3.0 models messier, real systems

Released July 28, the new demo adds agentic AI services, continuous profiling, mixed Prometheus and OpenTelemetry instrumentation, SQL query correlation, and an end-to-end test suite for traces, metrics, and logs. For SRE teams, it is a more credible sandbox for testing pipelines and failure modes before production.

Deployments · 21 Jul

AWS is making ECS control-plane actions inspectable

ECS Action Logs expose timestamped service-side operations during deployments and Managed Daemon updates. Logs can flow to CloudWatch Logs, S3, or Firehose; Amazon Q uses them to diagnose rollbacks and unstable revisions. Earlier in July, the ECS console also gained a live deployment timeline and health view.

AWS Action Logs announcement ↗ AWS deployment observability announcement ↗

03

Kubernetes · 14 Jul

Official project

Autoscaling signals and cluster state both get attention

Kubernetes’ July 14 guide demonstrates a custom metrics exporter for signals such as queue depth—useful when CPU and memory do not describe demand. On July 8, SIG etcd announced etcd 3.7, including the long-requested RangeStream feature. Together they highlight both ends of reliable orchestration: better workload decisions and a stronger state-store foundation.

Service signals · 6 Jul

CloudWatch links deployments to errors and latency

Application Signals Service Events automatically captures exception and latency snapshots plus deployment events for instrumented services. The useful operational shift is correlation: responders can ask whether a fresh change introduced a new symptom without first assembling the timeline by hand.

AWS Service Events announcement ↗

AI operations · 29 May

Google frames agents as a controlled SRE force multiplier

Google SRE describes agentic work across reliability design, production readiness, playbook maintenance, anomaly detection, investigation, and mitigation. Its stated boundary matters: higher-risk services and actions keep human review and control. The signal is broader than automated root-cause analysis; teams are testing agents across the full service lifecycle.
